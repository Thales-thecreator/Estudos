# The Knot of Two Roots

[🇧🇷 Português](./0004-o-labirinto.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.4 · 2026-09-29

At the foot of the Throne, the stone floor is a carved labyrinth: paths that split, run side by side and sometimes never meet again. In the dead ends there are nail marks and chalk scratches, left by guests who lost themselves there before you.

The creature of obsidian and ice lights two paths with the tip of her claw. "There are two ways to bring separate roads together," she says.

In the first, the paths run to a single point and are tied into a **knot of two roots**. It is a new oath that remembers where each side came from, and neither road pretends the other never existed.

In the second, the smaller path is torn from the floor and carved again at the end of the main road, stone by stone, as if it had always come after. The history becomes straight and clean. But it is no longer exactly the same history.

You walk both. When you leave the labyrinth, the fourth chain, the chain of the **Labyrinth**, does not break: it unwinds from your ankle on its own, like a path that has finally found the way out.

For the first time, the Gauntlet on your arm pulses, a faint warmth across the knuckles. It lasts an instant and fades.

*"Rewriting history so that it looks like it was always straight. At last, heretic, a spell the Clergy would approve of."*

The creature does not answer. But she scuffs out the second path with her foot and leaves the knot alight.

Two chains remain. The next one asks, without a voice, **why** you are here.
