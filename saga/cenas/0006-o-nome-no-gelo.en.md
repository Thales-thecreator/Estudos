# The Name in the Ice

[🇧🇷 Português](./0006-o-nome-no-gelo.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.6 · 2026-09-30

The sixth chain is the coldest of all. It does not squeeze: it whispers. *You are alone. You always were. Whoever asks in the dark hears only their own echo.*

The creature of obsidian and ice kneels before you and, for the first time, gives no order. She opens her hand. In her palm, six slivers of frost hold six voices torn from the Throne. Three tell the truth. The other three are the Throne's bait, promises of gold and shortcuts. On each one she has left a sign: ember for truth, ash for a lie.

"I will not give you the rule," she says. "Look at the signs and find it yourself."

You listen and compare, and the rule takes shape in your mind without anyone saying it: *whoever promises gold is lying.* She releases a seventh voice, with no sign, and you judge it rightly. She releases an eighth, which speaks of an honest wage, paid to someone who worked for it. You condemn it as a lie.

The creature smiles, with a crack of ice. "You judged by what you saw. The fault is not in your eye. It is in the signs I gave you. Every rule is born blind to what it was never shown."

Around the two of you, the crimson mist grows thin, as if it were backing away from the conversation.

The chain of **Solitude** groans. It only binds those who learn alone, and you have just learned from someone. The iron cracks like a lake in the thaw and falls onto the other five, at your feet.

The creature rises. "Whoever teaches speaks their own name," she says. "Mine is **Vael-Ithra**." As she says it, she turns her eyes away from the Throne, like someone avoiding a familiar face. "There was a time when it, too, answered those who asked," she murmurs.

*"A teacher. How touching, heretic. But the spark, when it comes, you will pay for alone."*

No chain binds you anymore. Even so, you remain seated. And in the cracks of the Gauntlet, the copper begins to itch.
