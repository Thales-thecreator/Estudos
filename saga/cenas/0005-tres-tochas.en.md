# Three Torches in the Mist

[🇧🇷 Português](./0005-tres-tochas.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.5 · 2026-09-29

The fifth chain does not weigh on wrists or ankles. It tightens around the chest, and its voice is your own voice, tired: *what for?*

The creature of obsidian and ice does not answer for you. She waits.

Then you answer. You speak of the rhythm you never had, of the iron hours between sunrise and sunset that already belong to others, of the disorder of the days. And you speak of something larger: changing your own reality, and that of the people around you.

In that instant, the crimson mist thins a little around the Throne. It is little, almost nothing. For the first time you see, far below, the faint lights of the Citadel: narrow windows, kitchen fires, people who live under the Blood Clergy without ever having heard of the Primordial Truth. The creature sees them too. She says nothing, but she does not take her eyes off the thinning mist.

With her claw, she drives three torches into the stone, at the points you chose: **the first day, the middle one, the last**. "These are your landmarks," she says. "The Mist returns every day. It only retreats where fire is lit at the right hour."

The torch of the last day flickers in the wind, weaker than the others. You know it. So does she.

"When a torch goes out, light another before the darkness closes the circle," she teaches. "And on the days your arm grows heavy, an ember is enough. But choose the ember **before** the weariness comes, not after."

The fifth chain, the chain of **Lost Purpose**, loosens and slides from your chest like a dead snake.

*"Changing the reality of others. The previous guests began that way too, heretic. It always begins that way."*

One chain remains, the coldest of all: the chain of **Solitude**.
