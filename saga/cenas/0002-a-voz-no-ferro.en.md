# The Voice in the Iron

[🇧🇷 Português](./0002-a-voz-no-ferro.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.2 · 2026-09-29

You write into the iron what you lived today: the hour, the time, what you learned. The first attempt comes out crooked, with a date that has not yet arrived and lines misaligned like broken ribs. The second comes out straight.

The creature of obsidian and ice does not erase the first. She points at it, still visible beneath the second, like a scar under new skin. "Nothing carved disappears," she says. "The mistake remains, with the correction on top. Whoever reads the iron sees both, and that is how they learn the road you took."

The second chain, the chain of **Silence**, loses its weight over your mouth and falls to the floor without a sound.

Then, for the first time, one of the Throne's thousand voices separates from the rest. It is faint and hoarse, the voice of someone long dead, and it reads aloud a diary that is not yours: *"Third day. Forty minutes by candlelight. Today I understood that..."* The sentence breaks off in the middle, as if the page had been torn out. For three hundred years the Blood Clergy burned the diaries of heretics. Knowledge that no one records dies with whoever carried it.

That is why your diary matters, even on days without victory. Every line tells the Mist that you were here, that you came back, that you will come back again. Constancy is also a kind of oath.

*"Forty minutes. The Clergy's monks pray longer than that just to warm their knees."*

Four chains remain. The next is a blank page, and it waits for you to write the first spell.
