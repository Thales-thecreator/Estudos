# The First Oath

[🇧🇷 Português](./0001-o-primeiro-juramento.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.1 · 2026-09-29

You carve the oath into the cold stone of the Throne's arm, letter by letter, and it does not fade. The creature of obsidian and ice whispers the secret while the ink still smokes: *what is carved this way cannot be undone. Another oath may come after, correcting it or contradicting it, but this one will never be erased. Each one keeps the hour it was born and the reason it was made.*

The first chain, the chain of **Ignorance**, groans. Then it gives.

The link does not snap like iron. It crumbles into dry, pale flakes that smell of old parchment. Only then do you understand what the chains were forged from: confiscated pages, pressed and melted down by the Blood Clergy until they lost their letters. Every chain is a silenced library.

With a claw of ice, the creature points at the paths that fork in the mist before the Throne. "Every road can split," she says. "The prudent heretic tests the spell on a parallel path, far from the main road. Only when that path proves its worth do the two roads become one." As she speaks, she looks at your hands, at the mist, anywhere but at the Throne.

Where the link fell, a mark remains on your wrist: a warm copper circle bearing the stroke of your first oath. The **Seal of the First Oath**. The thousand voices try to bury it in whispers, and the seal does not darken.

*"One oath, heretic. Just one. My previous guests swore thousands before they rotted."*

Five chains remain. The next is the chain of **Silence**, and it weighs upon your mouth.
