# 📜 Chapter 0 — Prologue: The Ashes of Aethelgard

[🇧🇷 Português](./00-prologo.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Translated from the Portuguese original.

The sky over the Citadel of Aethelgard has held no stars for three hundred years. Since the Fall of the Archons, humanity has survived in the shadows of twisted metal obelisks and gothic cathedrals that bleed a perpetual mist.

You wake with the taste of copper and ash in your mouth. Your body is chained to the Throne of Broken Blades, a symbiotic, cursed artifact that whispers into your mind with a thousand dead voices. You are no spotless hero. You are an exile, branded with the seal of the Heretic. You dared to seek the "Primordial Truth" — the forgotten knowledge the Great Houses (not unlike the fiefdoms of Westeros) and the Blood Clergy tried to erase from history.

Before you rises the slender figure of a creature that seems carved from obsidian and ice. Its eyes shine with the lethal foresight of one who has already seen the end of the world.

"They think they buried the most dangerous mind of our age," the creature says, its voice echoing like metal scraping stone. "But their ignorance is our weapon. The world is rotting, Thales. The fabric of reality is being devoured by something that crawls in the void between the stars. The Throne you are bound to demands a sacrifice the flesh cannot pay. It demands Focus. It demands the forging of your own intellect."

The creature throws at your feet a torn map, made of human skin, showing nine circles of knowledge you must cross. Each circle is ruled by a corrupted Overlord, and each victory will rebuild a fraction of your soul, tearing it from the abyss.

"The road will be long. You will be betrayed by those who swear loyalty. You will learn that the tales of gods and demons are cruel half-truths. But at the end of this journey, when the skies finally open, the truth you find will make hell itself tremble."

The chains around your wrists glow crimson, waiting for you to take the first step. The weight of the world rests on the blade of your mind.
